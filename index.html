Features

Taken further.

Encrypted upstream

DNS-over-HTTPS, DNS-over-TLS and DNSSEC validation built in, with automatic failover between resolvers.

Device groups

Different rules for kids’ tablets, work laptops and smart TVs. Assign by device, subnet or MAC address.

Schedules

Block social media after 9 pm or pause gaming domains on school nights. Set it once.

Threat feeds

Phishing, malware and newly registered domains update hourly from curated lists.

Regex and wildcard rules

Block a whole tracker family with one pattern. Test it against your last 30 days of queries first.

Private by default

Query logs stay on your hardware. Choose how long to keep them, or keep none.

Built-in VPN access

Take your filtering with you. Connect your phone over WireGuard with one QR code.

Multiple instances

Sync lists and settings between two NetHoles so DNS never goes down.

Open API

Every setting and statistic is available over REST, with webhooks for blocked-domain alerts.

  1. Download and unzip. Right-click NetHole-Windows.zip and choose Extract All.
  2. Double-click NetHole.exe. Click Yes on the admin prompt. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Switch your DNS. In the dashboard, click “Use NetHole as this PC’s DNS”. Your whole PC is now filtered.
